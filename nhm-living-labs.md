# NHM Living Labs

NHM Living Labs is aproject to convert the estates of the Natural History Museum into living laboratories. It has grown out of the transformation of the Museum's gardens in South Kensingotn as part of the [Urban Nature Project](/urban-nature-project).

It currently consists of the [Urban Research Station](/urban-research-station) at SOuth Kensington, with plans underway for the creation of a second living lab at Tring.
